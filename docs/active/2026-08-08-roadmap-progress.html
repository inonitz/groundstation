The honest cut for the POC

The demo can fly today: plan → takeoff → GO/APPROACH a real detected object → land, with a battery failsafe and a human override underneath it, all proven in SITL. The two things that most raise the ceiling in the time left are Spec 1's reactive safety (emergency boundary + interrupt) and Spec 2's drift-free GO. Everything in §7 (SLAM/OctoMap/A*) and the Tello hardware bring-up in §2.3 is realistically past this deadline. Before any real-Tello flight, the runtime-constants gap (9.14) must close — SITL numbers are not Tello numbers.

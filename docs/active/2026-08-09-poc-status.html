Can we fly the Tello tomorrow night?
Yes — a position-free VLM demo. Not autonomous movement.

The Tello reports no X/Y position, so anything that flies to a place needs SLAM, which is not ready. But everything that doesn't need position is proven on hardware already (telemetry, camera, planning, all confirmed live 2026-08-06). Keep focus on the position-free demo and a real flight tomorrow is realistic. Chase autonomous approach and you will spend the day on SLAM and fly nothing.

01

First thing in the morning — Tello runbook

Ordered because each step gates the next. Step 3 is the real gate: if bring-up is clean, the rest is tuning. Times are hands-on, gremlin budget included.

Land the two commits

Feature + governance, separately. Unblocks the FMU and gives you one binary to flash.

~15 min

Build the Tello target + write its run config

Fix camera resolution to the real Tello (~960×720), not the sim's 1280×720.

~30 min

Bring-up smoke test the gate

WiFi → command → streamon. Confirm the 16-field state parses, a camera frame decodes, and the 30 Hz rc keepalive stops the 15 s auto-land. Watch for UDP bind collisions (SO_REUSEADDR) and the ReceiveResponse timeout.

45–90 min

Takeoff · hover · land through the FMU

Confirm the drone holds station on the rc stream and lands cleanly. First real closed-loop moment.

~30 min

Camera → VLM planning loop

Confirm Qwen3-VL receives frames and emits plans off the live feed.

~30 min

Demo T1 — yaw-scan & describe

Takeoff, sweep with ROTATE, VLM narrates, land on command. Rough rc is fine; no calibration needed.

~60 min

Demo T2 — safety stack on hardware

Battery failsafe, manual override handoff, emergency stop. Sanity only, but it is the safest thing to show.

~30 min

Total hands-on ≈ 4–5.5 hours. A flight by night is realistic if the morning stays on this line. Buffer for WiFi/UDP gremlins, H264 decode hiccups, indoor prop-wash drift, and battery swaps (~10–13 min each — charge several).
